05Feature · Tipping points

Show where the answer flips, not how sure the AI is.

Problem

In chat, the honest answer is “it depends”. Users then have to guess on what, and by how much.

Design

Each driver carries its own threshold, drawn on the control. Moving it past the line flips the direction live, labels it What-if, and offers a way back to the real profile.

Why it builds trust

Direct manipulation turns “Maeve said so” into “I can see the rule”. Users learn which input actually matters for their case.

06Feature · How sure is this?

Every number says where it came from.

Problem

In a financial answer, a fact from Carta, an engine result and an AI guess all look the same. That’s where over-trust starts.

Design

Four epistemic states, one visual language. Colour carries the type; hover carries the source. Amber is reserved for AI estimates, so they can’t pass as calculations.

Builds on Trace

Maeve’s Trace already lets users follow the math. This moves it from a drill-down into the first thing users see, and leads with what still needs a check.

07Feature · Readiness

Say how finished the answer is, before the user acts.

Problem

A chat reply reads equally final whether it’s a first guess or an engine result. Users can’t tell when it’s safe to act.

Design

The workspace states its own maturity and always offers the one next step that moves it forward: answer, calculate, or save.

Ask less, ask better

Maeve only interrupts for unknowns that can flip the direction, and says why each one matters. Precision inputs stay folded away.

The saved record is why users come back, and why the next Secfi product feels like a continuation rather than a sale.

Claude Code turns a conversation into a working codebase. Maeve can turn it into a living decision model.

Why it matters for Secfi

A decision Maeve keeps watching gives users a reason to return when the 409A, a tender offer or their life changes, which are exactly the moments Secfi’s products are for.

How I’d validate it. A within-subject study, chat vs. workspace, 8–10 equity holders on the same exercise task. Measure mental-model accuracy (“what is Maeve assuming?”), appropriate reliance on a deliberately weak recommendation, and decision confidence. Success means better understanding without more blind trust.
